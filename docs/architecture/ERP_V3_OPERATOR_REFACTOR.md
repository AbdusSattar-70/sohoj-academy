Status: design and gap review, 2026-09-29. Branch: `feature/refactor`, based on `feature/finance_accounting_gap` at `4f45e0f`. This is a proposed V3 contract and migration plan, not an implemented or accepted V3 system.

The blueprint's North Star is one source of truth, traceable actions and software that guides the employee. It explicitly calls for canonical smart selects, a Prospect separate from Student, orchestrated Admission/Billing/Payment/Enrollment, localized updates, accessible forms and a new employee acceptance test. V3 retains these facts while changing how direct admission starts: a person who walks in to apply directly is not automatically a CRM enquiry.

## V3 domain contract

1. **Entry origin:** `DIRECT_STAFF`, `PUBLIC_APPLICATION`, `PROSPECT_CONVERSION`, or `EXISTING_STUDENT`. Direct intake has no Prospect. The optional `origin_prospect_id` references a real enquiry/application and has a controlled one-case conversion rule. Keep permanent prospect identity and timeline for actual Prospects.
2. **Applicant before acceptance:** one admission application with typed student particulars, verified guardian/contact relationship, source channel, school ID plus a deliberate historical name snapshot, academic intent and evidence. Draft changes are revisioned. Do not treat a draft as an ACTIVE Student or invoice.
3. **Existing identity:** search students, guardians and open applications by normalized mobile/name plus other identifiers. Show possible siblings separately; a match triggers human confirmation, reuse or controlled duplicate review. An existing Student retains its permanent ID.
4. **Case facts:** case links application, selected Offering and Batch, pinned Fee Plan version, referral choice, physical paper consent receipt, verification checklist, acceptance decision, Student, invoice and enrollment. Each fact has its own status/actor/time and policy reference. A case may have multiple events without flattening all facts into one status flag.
5. **Referral:** Organic or person ID; person may link staff/student/external contact. A new person is created once with normalized verified contact, duplicate screening and source evidence. The case stores the chosen ID and capture snapshot. Bonus remains contingent on collected tuition, eligibility and independent approval.
6. **Consent:** record physical signed form date, receiving staff and file reference; keep paper physically. Printing shows the exact case and fee snapshot. Receipt of paper is not a digital signature proof or a payment receipt.
7. **Money:** Fee Plan → assigned terms → initial/recurring invoice → actual payment/allocation → receipt → ledger. Approved discounts/credits/refunds remain separate facts. An unpaid invoice is receivable; activation follows the pinned policy and capacity guard.
8. **Security and history:** branch/organization scope, RLS, permissioned commands, idempotency and row locking, actor/correlation ID, maker-checker where required, immutable posted history and compensating records. No public account is required for student or guardian.

### Case workflow and screen behavior

| Stage | What staff sees and does on the case page | Completion evidence / gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start | Choose **New admission**, **Continue Prospect**, **Existing Student**, or **Print blank form**. Each opens its own route and returns to the case. | Source is explicit. Direct admission has no fake Prospect. |
| Review applicant | For Prospect, verify and correct identity first, then choose eligible offering and batch. For direct intake, enter once, review summary and use **Edit details** on the case if needed. | Verified fields, source record/revisions, duplicate review, applicable consent-to-contact recorded. |
| Placement and charges | Search eligible year/branch/class/offering; see published fees, schedule, seat count and why a choice is unavailable. Choose a batch and see next action. | Case pins Fee Plan; database rechecks eligibility and capacity at the relevant transition. |
| Referral | Select Organic, existing person/staff or **Add referrer** inline; new direct intake can finish this during entry. | Canonical referral ID or Organic, with verification attribution. |
| Paper consent | Print the case form; record guardian signature date and physical file location in the same step. | Stored receipt and staff attestation; no upload requirement. |
| Accept | One actionable **Accept admission** panel summarizes checked identity, placement, fees, referral and consent. Each check is explicit; exceptions ask for explanation. | Transaction issues/reuses Student ID and acceptance event, with audit; blocked checks show a direct fix action. |
| Bill, collect, enroll | Show pinned initial invoice preview and **Post bill**; on actual payment, inline **Record payment** using Finance; **Activate enrollment** or show precise policy/seat blocker. | Invoice, payment receipt and active enrollment remain distinct facts. A payment failure never fakes a receipt. |

The progress component is an ordered set of actual buttons/links, each with `complete`, `available`, or `blocked` state and accessible explanation. Clicking an available step opens its task panel and moves focus to its heading. It never silently advances or posts money. On success it shows the persisted result, updates the step and opens the next available task without losing the case context. Multiple previously completed facts remain reviewable.

### Less typing, clear control

- Search/select for year, branch, class, offering, batch, school, guardian relationship, referrer, payment method, reason code and relevant policy. Expose **Add new** only to roles allowed to create that particular master record.
- Create dialogs normalize names/mobile, show duplicate candidates, persist canonical IDs and return selection to the originating form. Inactive options remain readable in historical records. Fixed statuses and payment methods never have arbitrary create.
- Routine verification uses separate checks such as **identity confirmed with guardian**, **placement/seat reviewed**, **published fees explained** and **paper consent received**. Save exact check state, actor and date. Corrections, exceptions, overrides and rejections require a written reason; never substitute a checked box for authorization or required evidence.
- A valid option becoming unavailable between preview and submit produces a field-specific message and a recovery choice. Empty states name the exclusion (for example: “No active offering for Class 9 with an effective Fee Plan; correct the class or publish a plan”).

## Implementation sequence and V2 → V3 data transition

1. **Freeze and map V2 behavior.** Record the actual applied migration list, schema, row counts, current admission/prospect/student relationships, billing balances and open case statuses in a development clone. Fix any live P0 breakages independently while V3 is built. Establish representative cases: public Prospect, direct staff admission, existing Student, unpaid invoice, referral and legacy signed form.
2. **Design V3 baseline in isolation.** Draft ERD, SQL constraints, RLS, typed command contracts, permissions, event/audit rules and fee/accounting invariants. Build a fresh V3 development schema/database from one reviewed baseline plus new forward migrations. “From scratch” means clean architecture in development, never deletion of real V2 records or replay of 52 migrations against a blank production database.
3. **Additive bridge.** Keep the V2 ID/number maps. Add V3 application/origin fields and relationship tables through forward migrations to the existing database, or stage into a separate V3 database with verified crosswalks. Map synthetic ERP Prospects from `staff_admission_intake_requests` to `DIRECT_STAFF`, preserving their numbers/audit history as legacy links while excluding them from new lead conversion metrics. Real public/CRM Prospects keep their links. Do not guess unmarked historical origins; queue ambiguous cases for review.
4. **Build vertical slice.** Dedicated entry routes → eligibility query with reasons → case detail action panels → structured referral → physical consent → acceptance → invoice/payment/enrollment. Use canonical Finance commands and existing public catalogue contract. Keep V2 available during review; do not create competing financial truth.
5. **Reconcile and cut over deliberately.** Dry run migration twice, compare IDs/counts/fee versions/receivables/payables/journal balances, and run operator scenarios in a linked development Supabase. Take tested backup/restore, set a write cutover window, migrate deltas, verify reconciliations, then switch route and commands. Preserve V2 as read-only historical evidence until retention/rollback requirements are met. Publish a rollback decision that never drops posted data.
6. **Extend the interaction standard.** After admission passes, apply shared task panels, smart selects and localized refresh to offerings, Finance, academic reviews, student corrections and CRM. Each domain keeps its authority and approval rules.

## Acceptance gates for the first V3 release

- Direct staff admission creates **one** application/case and **zero** new Prospects; a real Prospect conversion retains its original Prospect ID and history. Duplicate and existing Student paths reuse/flag identities correctly.
- An old Prospect with a mismatched/stale class or offering gets an explanatory empty state and a permissioned correction path. After correction, eligible offerings and non-full batches appear. Database still rejects wrong offering, full batch and stale Fee Plan.
- Every start choice opens a focused form; a staff member can complete the normal case without hunting through other registers. Back/deep links preserve the case, keyboard focus and unsaved state where relevant.
- Steps are accessible actions. A new employee can explain the next task and blocker, verify evidence, record consent, accept, bill, collect real money and activate without developer help. No unsupported checkbox can bypass consent or approval.
- Referrer create/select is done once; duplicate mobile is reviewed; Organic is explicit; rewards are computed only under Finance policy and approvals.
- Fee plan, invoice, payment, receipt and journal totals reconcile to source facts; idempotent retries and concurrent last-seat/payment attempts cannot duplicate business events.
- Automated migration and rollback-only SQL scenarios, signed-in role/RLS integration, critical browser E2E, keyboard/touch, A4 printing and user acceptance with operator and independent approver pass. CI must run migration tests in addition to lint, typecheck and build.

## Immediate next implementation direction

Start with the **V3 admission origin and eligibility contract** in an isolated development database, plus a scripted V2 mapping report. Then implement the dedicated direct/Prospect entry routes and one case detail route against that contract. Do not rename the present V2 `prospect_id` to nullable or alter posted financial tables without proving conversion, history and reconciliation behavior first.
